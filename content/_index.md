I'm an astronomer. My main research interests are **cosmic reionization** and **galaxy evolution**.
I analyze large observational datasets from many telescopes, including **JWST**, the **Subaru Telescope** and the **VLT**,
using **big-data analysis** and **statistical inference**, and compare the results with **simulations and theoretical models**
to understand how the first galaxies and black holes formed.
I currently work at the Center for Computational Sciences, University of Tsukuba,
after receiving my Ph.D. from the University of Tokyo (ICRR / [Observational Cosmology Group](http://cos.icrr.u-tokyo.ac.jp)).

I'm enjoying my life right now. How about you?
